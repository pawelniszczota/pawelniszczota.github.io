Paweł Niszczota

Welcome!

I am an Associate Professor at the Poznań University of Economics and Business, and the head of the Humans & AI Laboratory (HAI Lab).

My research currently focuses mainly on large language models, but I also study sin stocks, behavioral finance, and judgment and decision making.
