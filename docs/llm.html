Large language models.

How do intelligent machines change cooperation, social norms, credibility, advice, and the choices people make?

National Science Centre, Poland

Botonomics

Botonomics: the influence of intelligent machines on economic behavior brings behavioral economics and experimental methods to questions emerging from human interaction with large language models.

The research presented on this page was financed by the National Science Centre, Poland.

Humans & AI Laboratory

Questions

What the work explores.

The program studies both how people behave toward AI systems and how they judge other people who use those systems.

Cooperation

Whether people cooperate with large language models—and how communication and prior human interaction shape that behavior.

Norms & delegation

Whether the same social norms apply when humans act directly or delegate decisions to large language models.

Credibility & advice

How people evaluate AI-assisted research, financial guidance, and dietary advice generated by conversational systems.
