Overview

Researching people and intelligent machines.

The Humans & AI Laboratory at the Poznań University of Economics and Business recruited two prospective PhD students for work on Botonomics: the influence of intelligent machines on economic behavior, financed by the National Science Centre, Poland through the SONATA BIS scheme.

Botonomics explores how large language models—including GPT, Claude, and Gemini—influence economic and human behavior. The project uses methods from behavioral economics and finance, including economic games and tasks that examine dishonest or risk-taking behavior.

Doctoral researchers were expected to help design and conduct online or laboratory experiments, analyze statistical data, co-author papers, and present findings at scientific conferences or workshops.

Eligibility

Requirements & key dates.

  • Candidates were required to hold—or be close to obtaining—a Master’s degree in a relevant discipline such as economics, finance, psychology, or computer science. Candidates from STEM disciplines and linguistics with exceptional qualifications were also considered.
  • Admission to the PUEB Doctoral School was a condition for receiving the NCN research scholarship.
  • The position required working from Poznań or travelling to Poznań whenever Doctoral School courses required attendance.
  • 15 Jul 2026Application deadline for the SONATA BIS scholarship.
  • 31 Jul 2026Deadline for applications to the PUEB Doctoral School.
  • 16 Sep 2026Confirmation of admission to the Doctoral School.
  • 1 Oct 2026Planned start of work.

Conditions

Two stipends in parallel.

Each doctoral student was to receive a Doctoral School stipend for up to 48 months and an NCN SONATA BIS stipend for 21 months, with a possible extension. At the rates stated in the archived call, combined monthly take-home pay was approximately 5,168 PLN during the first 21 months.

Doctoral School stipend

  • Approximately 3,570 PLN gross / 3,168 PLN net per month before the mid-study assessment.
  • Approximately 5,500 PLN gross / 4,881 PLN net per month after the assessment.

SONATA BIS stipend

  • 2,000 PLN net per month for 21 months.
  • Possible extension by a further 9–15 months.

Figures are retained from the original 2026 call for archival reference and may no longer reflect current rates.

Archived procedure

Application materials.

  1. A cover letter in English explaining interest in the project.
  2. A CV in English describing publications, project experience, and relevant skills.
  3. A copy of the Master’s diploma, or a declaration stating the expected graduation date.
  4. A declaration of intent to apply to the PUEB Doctoral School.
  5. Confirmation of admission to the PUEB Doctoral School at the later stage.

The process consisted of document assessment, an online interview, and parallel recruitment to the PUEB Doctoral School. The original National Science Centre listing is available in the NCN offer database.
