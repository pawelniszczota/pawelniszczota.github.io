Sin stocks & moral investment.

Why do people reject—or accept—investments in morally controversial companies, and what changes when machines enter the decision?

Methods

Questions

What the work explores.

The program links moral judgment with choices in finance, including attitudes toward industry, fund managers, and automated investment tools.

Moral boundaries

How deontological and utilitarian judgments predict disapproval of investing in morally controversial companies.

People & context

How gender, parenting, and the perceived character of decision-makers relate to judgments about sin stocks.

Machines in finance

How investors respond to robo-investment and to machines used to identify negative activist investments.
